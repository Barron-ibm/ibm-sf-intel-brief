Southern Glazer's Wine & Spirits
Private · Beverage distribution · About $26B in sales · Miami HQ · FTC deal filed Oct 2 (38 days ago), court approval still pending

This Week
Why Now — Nov 9, 2026
  • New this week: a Legal 500 article dated Nov 9 reviews the FTC settlement and how the Robinson-Patman Act is now being enforced. It confirms the order is still a proposed consent decree; no news of a judge's approval was found in Nov 3–9.
  • On Oct 2 (38 days ago) the FTC filed a proposed settlement with Southern Glazer's. It is the first Robinson-Patman win in 26 years. That law bans charging a small store more than a big chain for the same goods.
  • The rules are specific. For six years, in 26 states, the order watches pairs of sales: a chain buys a product, and within 45 days (75 for some chains) a nearby independent store buys a similar one at a much higher price. "Nearby" means 1.5 to 2.5 miles in cities, 6 miles in suburbs and 12 miles in rural areas. Twice a year, SGWS must hand its sales data to an independent monitor, and SGWS pays for the monitor.
  • If the monitor finds an overcharge, SGWS can fix it by paying the store 1.5 times the price gap. If it does not fix it and the FTC has to go to court, the payment goes to 2 times. That means SGWS has to check every pair of sales, all the time.
  • Competitor news, date not confirmed: Wine Spectator (Shanken News Daily) reports that RNDC and Breakthru Beverage, the No. 2 and No. 3 wholesalers, plan to merge into a company with about $14B in sales and about 25% market share, subject to regulatory approval, closing late in Q2 next year. We could not confirm when this was announced, and other sources describe RNDC as going through a Chapter 11 breakup with its markets sold off one by one. Treat it as a question to ask, not a fact to quote.
  • Oct 28 (before the window): Bacardi shifted its U.S. distribution. Reyes Beverage Group takes over 11 markets on Jan 1 (53 days away), including Florida. SGWS keeps 36 states and stays Bacardi's largest U.S. distributor by volume. Bacardi did not say who handled those 11 markets before, so we cannot say SGWS lost them. Note that other sources describe Reyes taking 11 former RNDC markets, so the source of the 11 markets is unclear.
  • Oct 28 (before the window): SGWS bought Clare Rose (Long Island) this spring, and its old East Yaphank warehouse was listed for sale while SGWS leases it for two years. More deals mean more systems to join up. Not re-verified: a California state review reported on Oct 9.
Hiring Signals
  • No dated job postings were verified in the Nov 3–9 scan. A company database shows about 382 open roles (undated, 17,226 employees), which is not a dated signal.
IBM Angle
SGWS now has to prove, twice a year for six years, that small stores are not overcharged. IBM watsonx and Data Fabric can match chain and independent-store sales automatically, flag a gap before it turns into a 1.5x payment, and keep the record the monitor will ask for. Planning Analytics then gives finance one view of price, margin and demand across states. Open with the General Counsel or Chief Compliance Officer, then bring in the CFO.
What to Say
